# Business Insights & Recommendations

## Key Business Insights

### 1. Basic Subscribers Have the Highest Churn Rate
Basic subscribers have the highest observed churn rate at **61.83%**, compared with 45.44% for Standard and 43.71% for Premium subscribers.

**Business implication:** The Basic subscription segment should be prioritized for retention analysis and targeted engagement strategies.

### 2. Crypto and Gift Card Users Show Higher Churn
Crypto users have the highest observed churn rate at **59.70%**, followed by Gift Card users at **57.79%**.

**Business implication:** These payment-method segments should be monitored to understand the factors associated with their higher churn.

### 3. Churned Customers Show Lower Viewing Engagement
Retained customers average **17.45 watch hours**, while churned customers average **5.92 watch hours**.

**Business implication:** Lower viewing engagement appears to be an important signal associated with customer churn.

### 4. Churned Customers Have Longer Login Gaps
Retained customers average **21.77 days** since their last login, compared with **38.31 days** for churned customers.

**Business implication:** Increasing inactivity could be used as an early warning signal for potential churn.

### 5. Device Type Has Limited Impact
Device-level churn rates range from **49.21% to 51.79%**, showing relatively small differences between devices.

**Business implication:** Device type appears less useful for churn segmentation than subscription type, payment method, and engagement behavior.

### 6. Overall Churn Is Significant
Out of 5,000 customers, **2,515 are churned**, resulting in an overall churn rate of **50.30%**.

**Business implication:** Customer retention should be treated as an important business priority.

## Recommendations

1. Target Basic subscribers with focused retention campaigns.
2. Monitor high-churn payment segments such as Crypto and Gift Card users.
3. Identify customers with declining watch activity.
4. Monitor increasing days since the customer's last login.
5. Use personalized content recommendations and engagement campaigns.
6. Continue monitoring churn patterns through the Power BI dashboard.
7. Use customer behavior and segmentation data to support targeted retention strategies.

## Conclusion

The analysis identified clear differences in churn across subscription types, payment methods, and customer engagement behavior. Basic subscribers showed the highest observed churn rate, while churned customers demonstrated substantially lower viewing activity and longer login gaps.

The combination of Python-based analysis and an interactive Power BI dashboard provides a practical approach for identifying high-risk customer segments and supporting data-driven retention decisions.

**Note:** The findings represent associations within the analyzed dataset and do not establish that any specific factor directly causes customer churn.
